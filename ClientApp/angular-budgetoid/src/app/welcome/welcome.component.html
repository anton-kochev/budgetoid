Always watching. Never judging.

One simple idea: decide what your money is for before you spend it.

Every bill, every goal, every ordinary week gets its share ahead of time. Bills stop being surprises, big goals get funded month by month, and “can I afford this?” always has a plain answer. Money stops being a background worry, and saving stops being an accident.

Your Google account is used only to sign you in.
