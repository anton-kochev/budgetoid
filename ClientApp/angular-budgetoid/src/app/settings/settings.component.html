Export

Download everything Budgetoid holds about you as one JSON file — your account, your budget, and every account, category group, category, payee and transaction in it.

The file is built and sent in the same request. There’s no queue, no email, and nothing to ask anyone for.

Erase everything

Erasing removes your account and everything in it — every budget, account, category, payee and transaction. There is no undo, and Budgetoid offers no way to bring an account back.

Erased data stays in point-in-time database backups for up to 7 days, and in no other place.

Erasing has to be confirmed with a passkey, and this screen doesn’t ask for one yet. The button stays off until it does.

What we can read

Budgetoid’s operators can read everything you record: amounts, dates, currency codes, account types, the order you arrange things in, the timestamps on every row, the identifiers behind them, and your email address. Today that also includes the names and notes you type. Nothing is encrypted with a key only you hold — not yet.
